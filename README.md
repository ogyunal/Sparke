# ⚡ Sparke  
*Built in 2019 — three years before BeReal told everyone to “be real.”*

Sparke was a social platform **way ahead of its time**.  
Think Snapchat meets [BeReal](https://bere.al) — except **Sparke existed before BeReal did**.  
The idea? You had to post *once a day*. No filters, no flexing, just pure, unfiltered (okay, slightly filtered) daily life.

Unfortunately, we didn’t raise $90M. We just raised our hopes.  

---

## 💡 Introduction

Welcome to **Sparke** — the forgotten ancestor of the “authentic social media” movement.  
In **2019**, while BeReal was still in stealth or maybe in someone’s notes app, Sparke already encouraged users to post once a day and share something real.

Our mission: keep people connected without endless scrolling.  
Our fate: being too early. And too broke.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Daily Posts:** One post per day — the rule we made before it became a trend.  
- **Stories:** Photos, videos, and text updates — minus the pressure.  
- **Friends & Followers:** Because “being real” is more fun with an audience.  
- **Chat:** For when you actually want to talk instead of scroll.  
- **Notifications:** Gentle reminders to “be real” — before it was trademarked.  

---

## 🕰️ Legacy

Sparke launched quietly in **2019**, then quietly disappeared — like an introvert at a networking event.  
Three years later, [BeReal](https://bere.al) came along and made billions with a camera flip.  
We’re not bitter. Just historically correct.
